# Claude Telegram Bridge 0.14.18

- The Stop button now also appears on the work-flow card of a turn that was started in the terminal. Until now the bridge attached the button only to the card of a turn that started from the phone; a turn typed at the keyboard produced the same work-flow card but offered no way to interrupt it from Telegram. With the flow mirror on, the card of a terminal-started turn carries the Stop button from its first tool step, keeps it across updates, and loses it when the bridge closes the card (final answer, or a new turn taking over). Tapping it checks that the same native turn is still running and sends one Escape to the Claude session, exactly as for a phone-started turn; a tap on a card whose turn has already ended is answered with "already ended" and nothing is sent.
- Phone-started turns are unchanged: while a phone turn is active its card owns the button, and a terminal card never takes it over. With the flow mirror off, nothing changes.

Validation: new unit coverage for the terminal-started card (button on create and on update, one Escape per tap, retirement on close and on the next terminal turn, phone-turn precedence, no button when the bout is closed or has no origin), three mutation probes against the owner, close and new-turn paths, the existing stop-button, flow-card and export suites, the public export with its undefined-global gate and module parity check, private-data and token scans compared against the previous public tree, release-notes lint, twine check, and an installed-wheel resource check in an isolated environment. Source bundle, wheel and source distribution are included.

This release is published on GitHub and PyPI. It does not restart an installed bridge; after `pipx upgrade claude-telegram-bridge`, restart the bridge yourself.
